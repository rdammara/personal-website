# Design direction

Written from Albert's answers on 2026-10-14. This is the direction the antislop filter applies on top of.

## Identity

- Owner: Albert Richard Dammara
- Role: data engineer for industrial and IoT data. Background in Information Systems (minor in Big Data Analytics), industrial manufacturing data and industrial analytics. Currently a Software Quality Engineer.
- Domain: albertdammara.com (bought, not yet pointed at this server)
- Name: always written in full, "Albert Richard Dammara", including next to the logo.
- Logo: a dark-blue hexagon with an "A" cut through it and three orange bars inside. On dark backgrounds the hexagon turns light. Waiting on the original SVG file.

## Purpose and audience

- A personal portfolio for work and business. The main readers are employers and clients.
- A personal blog for posts about anything, mostly about work.

## Mood

- Calm and editorial, with a light touch of Apple's site as inspiration.
- Fast. Restraint over effects: few fonts and weights, no heavy animation.
- Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Palette

Albert's favourite colours: dark blue and orange. Dark blue is the ink and the brand surface; orange is the one accent, used sparingly.

| Token | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #FFFFFF | #0B1730 |
| Surface | #F3F5F9 | #12213F |
| Ink (text, brand) | #0E2245 | #EEF1F6 |
| Muted text | #4B5568 | #A9B4C7 |
| Accent text and links | #B4480C | #F5A05F |
| Accent fill (text on it is the background ink) | #F2913D | #F5A05F |
| Border | #D5DBE5 | #24365A |

Every text pairing above was checked with the antislop contrast checker and passes WCAG AA for normal text.
